## CORE FOCUS

### System Architecture & Role Hierarchy

The system operates as a true agentic swarm — a self-organising network of role-differentiated drones governed by decentralised AI logic, with each unit executing its specialised function autonomously while remaining coordinated through the overwatch layer. The swarm comprises three primary roles:

---

#### The Mothership (Overwatch Unit)

A lighter-than-air blimp-style platform that acts as the persistent command and intelligence hub. By leveraging buoyancy rather than active thrust, the mothership minimises power consumption and maintains a stable, elevated position well above the threat plane.

**Sensors & Capabilities:**
- LiDAR for spatial mapping
- Infrared thermal imaging
- AI-powered visual recognition for real-time object classification
- Continuous logging into a structured incident database
- Timestamped evidence records and time-lapse imagery suitable for formal incident reports, insurance claims, and criminal prosecution support

---

#### The Scout (Recon Unit)

A compact, ultra-low-profile drone engineered for minimal acoustic and radar signature. 

**Functions:**
- Autonomous geofence sweeping on scheduled patrol cycles
- Threat confirmation on triggered alerts
- Live user-viewable reconnaissance
- Cross-reference events against the system's threat database
- Escalate unresolved events for human verification or log as new unknown-threat profiles for future learning

---

#### The Soldier (Tactical Response Unit)

A modular response drone with a fixed, aerodynamic disc-profile body optimised for slingshot launch.

**Interchangeable Payload Modules:**
- Directional strobe and high-decibel siren, AI-targeted to the confirmed threat
- Non-lethal deterrent spray (pepper spray or equivalent humane compound)
- UV-ink mist deployment — covertly marking a subject without their awareness, enabling forensic tracking and post-incident evidence collection

---

### Launch & Deployment Infrastructure

The ground docking station serves as both a wireless charging hub and a rapid-deployment launcher. 

**Key Features:**
- 360° tower rotation, acquiring target bearing data from perimeter-mounted ultrasonic and motion sensors
- Once target is locked, tower rotates to intercept angle and launches 3–4 drones in rapid, timed succession
- Each drone follows a pre-calculated flight arc
- Soldier drones maintain compact disc-profile until launch arc is achieved, maximising slingshot efficiency before transitioning to active flight mode
- Mothership can be tethered aloft using a low-power box-kite structural design
- Soldier drone optionally tasked with mid-air wireless power relay during extended tracking operations

---

### Adaptive Patrol & Response Logic

The system operates on layered trigger logic:

| Mode | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Routine** | Scheduled | Scout sweeps geofence zones, builds behavioural baseline |
| **Alert** | Perimeter sensor | Scout confirms event classification against threat database |
| **Response** | Confirmed threat | Soldier deployment via launch tower; mothership coordinates and logs |
| **Escalation** | Unclassified/persistent threat | Human operator notified with live visual feed for manual decision |

All events — patrol logs, triggered responses, sensor data, and visual records — feed into a centralised incident database structured for legal evidentiary standards.

## NOVELTY & EXPANSION

### Platform Extensibility (IFTTT Logic)

As drone hardware costs continue to fall, the role-variable swarm architecture becomes a general-purpose automation platform. Using open agentic orchestration frameworks (such as OpenAI's Swarm, Hermes, or equivalent), virtually any household or property task can be assigned to a purpose-configured drone unit. The system's modular payload design means new roles are added by swapping hardware and registering a new agent profile — not rebuilding the system.

#### Mail & Parcel Sorting Agent

- Correctly addressed mail routed to resident's preferred drop point (e.g., business mail to workshop, personal mail to front door)

- Mis-addressed mail for neighbours automatically rerouted to a pre-agreed community exchange point
- All actions logged and timestamped
